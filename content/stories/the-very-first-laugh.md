# The Very First Laugh 😆💛

**A Mini Moments Matter Story**
*Series: Baby Moments (0–12mo) — for the grown-up who notices
Draft by: Content team — original unpublished draft, 2026*

---

It was just another ordinary morning.

The sun was soft through the window.

The coffee was warm.

The house was quiet in that busy, tired, wonderful way of a house with a baby in it.

You were about to do the ordinary things.

The nappy. The bottle. The tiny pile of washing.

And then it happened.

You leaned over the cot to say good morning.

You made a sudden little sound.

Boop!

One little finger. One little nose.

A face peered up at you.

And then...

The breathless little giggle.

Not a smile. Not a gurgle. Not a sleepy sneeze.

A laugh.

A real laugh.

A tiny, breathless, whole-body giggle that lit up the whole room.

You froze.

Did you hear that?

You looked.

The little eyes were bright. The little mouth was open. The little body was shimmering with joy.

Somewhere in that tiny chest, the very first laugh had burst out into the world.

And you were the one who caught it.

You wanted to make it happen again.

So you did the same thing.

Boop!

Another little nose.

Another little giggle.

Boop!

A bigger laugh.

The sound travelled.

It tumbled out of the bedroom.

It bounced down the hallway.

Somewhere in the house, the grown-ups heard it.

"What was that?"

They came. They leaned in. They listened.

"What's all this?" the other grown-up asked, peeking in.

And you did it again.

Boop!

And the little laugh came out again, brighter and braver.

And the house... tried to make the magic happen again.

The grown-ups clapped.

The grown-ups sang.

The grown-ups made silly faces.

The grown-ups said "boop" in every voice they owned.

They took turns leaning in, hoping to catch it, hoping to be the one who made the magic happen.

Boop!

Giggle.

Boop!

Giggle.

Boop!

A yawn.

The little eyes went soft.

The little body relaxed.

The laughing was finished for now.

The moment had passed.

And that was okay.

Because you had been there for the very first one.

You had heard the very first sound of this tiny person discovering that joy could burst out of them.

That the world could be funny.

That someone was going to play with them, and love them, and come when they laughed.

The little laugh didn't happen on a schedule.

It didn't arrive with instructions.

Nobody planned it.

It simply arrived, sudden and bright, on an ordinary morning.

And you caught it.

That's the thing about being a grown-up who notices.

You were there for a first that nobody else in the whole world will ever hear the same way.

The very first laugh.

And you were the one who made it happen.

What makes you laugh?

What silly sound do you make together that's just yours?

Write it down. Say it out loud. Tell someone about the moment you heard the first real laugh.

Because there is a "first time everything" happening in your house right now.

And you are the one who gets to notice.

## 😄 Let's Play the Boop-and-Giggle Game

Ready? It's simple. And it might not work.

That's the point.

Here's how to play:

👋 Sit close, face to face, on the floor or on your lap.
👆 Say "boop!" and give a gentle, silly tap on the nose. Or the cheek. Or the chin.
😄 Watch what happens.
😮 Try it a different way: slower, bigger, whispery, high, low.
🎶 Add a song. Make the boop part of a tune.
👨‍👩‍👧 Pass the baby from grown-up to grown-up, each taking a turn to make the giggle happen.

Notice what works today.

Notice what doesn't.

Some days the laugh comes easy. Some days the baby is busy looking at their toes instead.

That's okay. The game isn't about making the laugh happen.

The game is about being together, looking at each other, and playing.

The laugh is just the treasure you might find along the way.

## 🌱 As You Grow...

When your baby is tiny, the laugh sounds like a breathless little gasp.

As they grow, the laugh changes.

It becomes a baby belly laugh.

Then a toddler's forgetting-about-it-laugh, all the way from the other room.

Then a child's joke-telling laugh.

Then a school-day laugh that comes home in the car.

The laugh keeps changing.

But the feeling — the burst of joy that something is funny, that someone is safe to laugh with, that the world is full of surprises — that stays the same.

And one day, you might tell them the story of the very first laugh.

You will be the keeper of that memory.

The one who heard it first. Who caught it. Who remembers the light through the window.

That is why this moment is worth keeping.

You are not just remembering a sound.

You are remembering the beginning of their joy.

## 😆 The Tiny Moment

It wasn't planned.

It wasn't on any schedule.

It wasn't on the to-do list that morning.

It was simply... a sudden little sound.

A breathless giggle at a gentle boop.

But someone was paying attention.

Someone stopped. Someone looked. Someone caught it.

And for a moment, a very ordinary morning turned into the day the very first laugh happened.

That's the magic of being the grown-up who notices.

The biggest first moments often arrive without any warning.

They just need someone there to see them.

You were there.

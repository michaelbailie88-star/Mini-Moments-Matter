# The Tiny Things 💛

**A Mini Moments Matter Story**
*Series: Curious Moments (2–3) / Big Little Adventures (3–5) / Growing Minds (5–7+) — kindness & connection
Draft by: Owner — original unpublished draft, 2026*

---

Every morning, Lily arrived at school with something important to do.

She would put her little things away, find her friends, and say hello to her teachers.

But before the morning could really begin, Lily had one very special mission.

She had to find a flower.

It didn't have to be big.

It didn't have to be perfect.

Sometimes it was yellow.

Sometimes it was white.

Sometimes it was so tiny that you almost didn't notice it.

But Lily noticed.

And when she found one, she would carefully pick it up and carry it inside.

Then she would walk over to her teacher, hold out her tiny treasure with both hands, and smile.

"Here you go."

Her teacher would smile too.

"Thank you, Lily."

And Lily would go about her day.

The next morning, she did it again.

A little flower.

A little smile.

A little gift.

Every day, Lily brought her teachers a flower.

And every day, her teachers treasured it.

They began to wonder what Lily would find next.

Would it be yellow?

Would it have lots of petals?

Would it be tiny enough to fit between two little fingers?

Sometimes Lily would tell them why she chose a particular flower.

"Because it's pretty."

"Because it's yellow."

"Because I found it!"

And sometimes she didn't say anything at all.

She simply handed it over.

One morning, Lily came inside carrying a particularly tiny flower.

She walked carefully across the room.

"Teacher," she said, holding it out, "this is for you."

Her teacher looked at the little flower.

It was small.

Very small.

But somehow, it didn't feel small at all.

Because Lily had thought about her.

She had noticed something beautiful.

She had wanted to share it.

And she had taken the time to bring it to someone she cared about.

Her teacher smiled and held the flower carefully.

"Thank you, Lily. You always know how to make someone feel special."

Lily smiled.

Then she went off to play.

She probably didn't realize that her little flowers had become something much bigger.

They had become part of her teachers' mornings.

A little reminder to slow down.

A little reminder to notice.

A little reminder that kindness doesn't have to be big to matter.

Sometimes kindness looks like a tiny hand holding a tiny flower.

Sometimes love sounds like: "I found this for you."

And sometimes the smallest things leave the biggest marks on our hearts.

## 🌼 Mini Moment Reflection

**Talk Together**

Ask your child:

- What do you think Lily liked about giving flowers to her teachers?
- How do you think her teachers felt when Lily brought them a flower?
- Have you ever given someone something because you thought it would make them happy?
- What is something small that makes you happy?
- Can something tiny be very important? Why?

**💛 Lily's Little Question**

If you could give someone one tiny treasure today, what would you give them — and why?

## 🌱 Let's Try It Together: Tiny Treasures

Go outside together and look for something tiny that catches your attention.

It could be:

🌼 A fallen flower
🍂 A tiny leaf
🪨 A little stone
🌿 A piece of grass
🌰 A small seed
🐚 A tiny shell

Take a moment to look closely.

What makes it special?

Why did you notice it?

If appropriate, leave living plants where they are and collect only fallen natural treasures.

Then ask:

"What makes something special even when it's small?"

## 🌼 As You Grow...

One day, Lily may stop bringing flowers.

She may become fascinated by bugs.

Or rocks.

Or clouds.

Or music.

Or space.

Or something completely unexpected.

And that's okay.

Because the treasure was never really the flower.

The treasure was Lily noticing something beautiful and wanting to share it with someone she loved.

That little instinct — to notice, to wonder, to give, to connect — can grow right alongside her.

Today, it might be a flower.

Tomorrow, it might be a drawing.

One day, it might be a thoughtful note, a helping hand, an idea, or a kind word.

The things children bring us change as they grow.

But the little moments of connection?

Those are worth keeping forever.
